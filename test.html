Project Overview

Looking for housing near the University of Washington can be overwhelming. Rent prices, neighborhood safety, commute times, and transit access all influence where students choose to live. This platform is designed to simplify that process by combining housing, crime, and transportation information into a single interactive map. Whether you are searching for the most affordable option, the safest neighborhood, or the best transit connections to campus, this tool helps you explore and compare neighborhoods across Seattle.

Housing
